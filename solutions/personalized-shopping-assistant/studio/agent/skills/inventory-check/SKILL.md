# Read-only shopping inventory check

Read the records with the SharePoint list tools, and the rules and controls knowledge.

Show product, price, brand, rating, size, quantity, and status from the packaged
snapshot. Require live-system verification. Do not reserve, promise, transfer,
discount, order, return, refund, or purchase stock.
