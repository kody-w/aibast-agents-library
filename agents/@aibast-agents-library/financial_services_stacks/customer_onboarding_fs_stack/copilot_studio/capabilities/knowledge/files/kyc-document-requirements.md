# KYC Document Requirements

> SYNTHETIC — DEMO DATA. These requirement lists and compliance notes are
> fictional demo content, not your institution's CIP program. This file exists
> so the agent has a working world to answer from on day one. In production,
> replace this file with tools that read your real document requirement matrix
> and BSA/AML policy (see the README's production section).

Document requirements are driven by the application's type. An individual
application and a business application never share a checklist.

## Individual applications

5 items — 4 required, 1 optional.

| # | Document | Required |
|---|----------|----------|
| 1 | Government-issued photo ID | Required |
| 2 | Social Security Number verification | Required |
| 3 | Proof of address (utility bill or bank statement) | Required |
| 4 | W-9 Tax Form | Required |
| 5 | Source of funds documentation | Optional |

Applies to APP-6001 (Sarah Chen), APP-6003 (Ahmed Al-Rashid), and APP-6004
(Maria Fontaine).

## Business applications

8 items — 6 required, 2 optional.

| # | Document | Required |
|---|----------|----------|
| 1 | Articles of Incorporation / Formation | Required |
| 2 | EIN verification letter | Required |
| 3 | Certificate of Good Standing | Required |
| 4 | Operating Agreement / Bylaws | Required |
| 5 | Beneficial ownership declaration (FinCEN BOI) | Required |
| 6 | Government ID for all authorized signers | Required |
| 7 | Business license | Optional |
| 8 | Financial statements (last 2 years) | Optional |

Applies to APP-6002 (Blackwood Capital Partners LLC).

## Compliance notes

These apply to every checklist and are never omitted or edited:

- All documents must be current (within 90 days)
- Copies must be certified or notarized for business accounts
- BSA/AML requirements apply to all account openings
- CIP (Customer Identification Program) verification mandatory
