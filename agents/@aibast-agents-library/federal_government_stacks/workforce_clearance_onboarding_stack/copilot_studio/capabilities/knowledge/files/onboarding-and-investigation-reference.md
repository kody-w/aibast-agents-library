## Investigation timeline reference

Target days is the gate the tracker measures against. An investigation is
flagged overdue only when days elapsed exceed the target AND the clearance is
not yet active.

| Tier | Name | Avg Days | Target Days |
|------|------|----------|-------------|
| T1 | Tier 1 (Low Risk) | 30 | 40 |
| T2 | Tier 2 (Moderate Risk / Public Trust) | 60 | 80 |
| T3 | Tier 3 (Secret) | 90 | 120 |
| T4 | Tier 4 (High Risk Public Trust) | 120 | 150 |
| T5 | Tier 5 (Top Secret / SCI) | 180 | 240 |

## Onboarding phases

Four phases, always processed in this order. A phase carries one status
(`complete`, `in_progress`, `pending`) for the whole phase — individual steps
are not tracked separately.

### Pre Arrival

| Step | Required | Days Before EOD |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| Tentative offer accepted | Yes | 30 |
| SF-86 submitted to DCSA | Yes | 28 |
| Drug test completed | Yes | 21 |
| Official offer letter issued | Yes | 14 |
| PIV card pre-enrollment | Yes | 7 |

### Day One

| Step | Required |
|------|----------|
| Oath of office administered | Yes |
| PIV card issued and activated | Yes |
| Building access badge provisioned | Yes |
| IT equipment issued (laptop, phone) | Yes |
| Orientation briefing attended | Yes |

### First Week

| Step | Required |
|------|----------|
| Network account activated | Yes |
| Email and collaboration tools provisioned | Yes |
| Mandatory cyber awareness training | Yes |
| Records management training | Yes |
| Meet with supervisor — IDP discussion | Yes |

### First 30 Days

| Step | Required |
|------|----------|
| Complete all required TMS training modules | Yes |
| Ethics briefing and financial disclosure (if applicable) | No |
| Telework agreement signed | No |
| Benefits enrollment confirmed | Yes |
| Performance plan established | Yes |
