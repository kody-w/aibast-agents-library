Publish your own agents

Your username is your publisher handle. Everything you ship appears here under your own track, with its own review thread and its own numbers.

Step 1 — Apply once

Claim your publisher track

A two-minute form registers your handle and what you plan to publish. Use the GitHub link in the header to start it.

Step 2 — Write one file

One agent, one self-contained file

No project scaffolding and no build step. A single file describes what the agent is called, what it does, and how to run it — and it is checked automatically before it goes live.

Step 3 — Ship it

Published means live everywhere

Once accepted, your agent appears in this gallery, gets its own review thread, and starts counting on the metrics dashboard.
