Publish your own agents

Your GitHub username is your publisher handle — everything you ship lives under agents/@<your-github-username>/ and appears here under your own track, with its own rating thread and metrics.

Step 1 — Apply once

Claim your publisher track

A two-minute issue form registers your handle (defaults to your GitHub username) and what you plan to publish.

Become a publisher
Step 2 — Write one file

agent.py or skill.md — your choice

A single self-contained file with a __manifest__ naming it @<your-username>/<slug>. CI validates the manifest on every pull request.

Read the publishing guide
Step 3 — Open a PR

Merged = live everywhere

On merge the registry rebuilds, your agent appears in this gallery, its Discussions rating thread is seeded automatically, and it starts counting on the metrics dashboard.
